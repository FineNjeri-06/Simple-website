ABOUT FUTURE SKILLS TRAINING CENTRE

Future Skills Training Centre is a leading institution of higher learning in Kenya, offering a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various fields. Our mission is to provide quality education that equips students with the knowledge and skills needed to succeed in their chosen careers.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution of higher learning in Kenya, recognized for excellence in education, research, and innovation.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that equips students with the knowledge and skills needed to succeed in their chosen careers, while fostering a supportive and inclusive learning environment that encourages innovation, critical thinking, and personal growth.

Core values

  • Excellence in education, research, and innovation
  • Integrity and ethical conduct
  • Inclusivity and diversity
  • Collaboration and partnerships
  • Lifelong learning and personal growth
